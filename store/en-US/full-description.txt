Place, match and merge your way to a bigger number in Merge Seven, a relaxing hexagon puzzle with satisfying chain reactions.

Tap or drag a piece onto the board. Rotate it to find a better fit. Connect three or more tiles with the same number to merge them into the next doubled value. Plan your space, set up another match and watch your chain grow.

SIX WAYS TO PLAY
• Campaign: work through levels with clear goals and earn stars.
• Endless: keep merging and challenge your personal best.
• Time Attack: make the most of the countdown.
• Zen: enjoy a calmer puzzle with unlimited undo.
• Daily: try a fresh number puzzle each day.
• Weekly: explore a changing personal challenge.

A COMFORTABLE PUZZLE TABLE
Bright hexagon tiles, readable numbers and a warm wood board make each move easy to follow. Placement previews help you find a fit before committing. Short merge animations and gentle original music add a little satisfaction to every chain.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Resume your saved game from Home. Collect coins through play, claim daily rewards and spend earned coins on boosters and cosmetics. Learn placement, rotation and merging through a short playable tutorial.

MAKE IT YOURS
Adjust music, sound effects and haptics separately. Choose reduced motion, color accessibility options and larger touch targets. The layout adapts to phones and tablets. Enable an optional local daily reminder whenever you want an invitation back to the puzzle.

OFFLINE PLAY, NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
All six normal game modes are available offline. Progress is saved on your device. There is no sign-in, leaderboard, subscription or in-app purchase.

The app contains ads. Optional rewarded ads explain their benefit before you choose them and require an internet connection. Menu banners and occasional ads between successful campaign levels support the game. Normal play does not require watching an ad.
